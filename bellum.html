Bellum is a next-generation, platoon-scale tactical shooter set in a fractured Africa of the 2030s — a world where global powers fight their wars by proxy, and three asymmetric factions collide across 4×4 km battlespaces built for authentic, consequential combat. My work focuses on building the environments that ground that fiction: the jungles, savannas, and broken urban sprawl that eighty players call the frontline.

Every space is designed to read instantly and survive scrutiny at close range — sun-bleached village schools, collapsed compounds, and overgrown treelines that offer cover without ever feeling arbitrary. Terrain, foliage, and structure all have to serve the tactics: sightlines, chokepoints, and flanking routes are as much a level-design concern as an art one.

Built in Unreal Engine 5 with Lumen and Nanite, the environments lean on dense geometric detail and dynamic lighting to sell scale and heat without sacrificing the readability a platoon-scale firefight demands.
