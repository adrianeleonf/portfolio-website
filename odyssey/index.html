Odyssey VR

VR Experience - Unreal Engine

Overview

࿇

Odyssey is a virtual reality project I worked on as lead environment artist — a psychological horror experience set aboard a station that's been dark for years. The objective is to complete a set of tasks scattered through its corridors in order to reach the escape pods and leave.

One of the biggest challenges we faced was performance. VR demands a much higher, more consistent frame rate than a standard game, so the environments leaned on modular kits, tileable textures, and trim sheets to keep everything lightweight without losing the density of detail the horror needed.
